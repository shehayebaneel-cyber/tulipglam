Plain text. Read it on your phone. Nothing here is live.

Five things need your answer. They are all in section 0. Everything after that is

===============================================================================
0. THE FIVE ANSWERS
===============================================================================

1) RETURNS WINDOW — 7 days or 14?

   This one has code behind it. The loyalty programme already assumes seven:
   points confirm 7 days after delivery, and the whole reason for that hold is
   that a cash-on-delivery order can come back.

   If you say 14, a customer can return on day 10 having already spent the points
   that order earned. I would then change the hold to 14 to match, which means
   everyone waits two weeks to spend points.

   7 is the safer launch number. You can extend it later; shortening a returns
   window after launch is the move that upsets people.

   MY RECOMMENDATION: 7.


2) ARE OPENED COSMETICS RETURNABLE?

   MY RECOMMENDATION: no, on hygiene grounds, WITH an exception if the fault is
   ours — damaged, faulty, or not what was ordered. Without that exception
   written down, whoever answers the WhatsApp will improvise it differently
   every time, and the second customer will hear about the first one's outcome.


3) DELIVERY TIMES — Beirut, and outside Beirut?

   The one number a customer most wants and the one I cannot measure for you.
   The site used to say "2-5 working days"; that was removed because nothing
   backed it, and I will not put it back as a guess.

   How long does it actually take you to source an item and get it to a door?
   Give me a range for Beirut and a range for elsewhere.


4) THE NAME THAT RUNS THE BUSINESS

   A privacy policy needs someone accountable. "TulipGlam" as a trading name is
   fine if that is what it is. If there is a registered company, that name goes
   here instead.


5) PRIVACY CONTACT — WhatsApp only, or an email too?

   WhatsApp is what your customers actually use. It is also unusual on a privacy
   page, and a minority of people will not trust a policy with no email address
   on it. supportEmail is currently unset.

1. RETURNS
===============================================================================

   Returns

   If something isn't right, tell us within [7 / 14] days of delivery and we'll
   put it right.

   Because we sell cosmetics and personal care, we can only accept items back
   unopened and unused, with their seal intact. That is a hygiene rule, not a
   technicality — we cannot resell an opened product, and neither would you want
   us to.

   This does not apply if we got it wrong. If an item arrives damaged, faulty,
   or isn't what you ordered, we replace or refund it whether it is opened or
   not. Message us on WhatsApp with your order number and a photo and we'll sort
   it out.

   Refunds are made in cash, by the same route the order was delivered, or as
   store credit if you prefer. Since orders are paid on delivery, there is no
   card to refund to.

   Points earned on a returned order are removed with it.

   ---
   Note: that last line is already true in the code — a reversal claws the
   points back and removes the order from tier progress. It is written here so a
   customer meets it in the policy rather than in an argument.

2. DELIVERY
===============================================================================

   Delivery

   We deliver across Lebanon. The delivery fee and the free-delivery threshold
   are set in your Settings and are already live on the site, so this paragraph
   fills itself in.

   Because we source each order rather than holding stock, we confirm every item
   with you on WhatsApp before it goes out. Most orders reach you within
   [ ] — needs your real range, e.g. "2-4 days in Beirut, 3-6 days elsewhere".

   Pay when it arrives. Cash on delivery, in US dollars. Please have the amount
   ready — the driver may not carry change.

   If an item turns out to be unavailable, we contact you before dispatching
   anything. You can swap it, drop it, or cancel the whole order.

   ---
   Note: keep the line about change. It is the single most common friction in
   cash-on-delivery, and saying it up front prevents an argument at a door —
   which now means your own door-step, since you are delivering these yourself.

3. PRIVACY
===============================================================================

   Privacy

   [ ] runs TulipGlam — needs your legal or trading name.

   If you want to know what we hold about you, or want it deleted, message us on
   WhatsApp at [your number] [or email — only if you want one] and we'll deal
   with it.

   What we keep, and why

   When you order we keep your name, phone number, delivery address and — if you
   give one — your email, because we cannot deliver an order or tell you it has
   shipped without them. If you make an account we also keep your password,
   stored scrambled in a way that cannot be reversed, and your order history.

   If you join TulipGlam Rewards we keep your phone number as the thing your
   points attach to, and your birth month if you give it — the month only, never
   the date, because a birthday perk needs to know when, not how old.

   If you left your email on our coming-soon page, we keep it to tell you when
   we open. Nothing else. Reply to any message and we'll remove you.

   What we don't do

   We don't sell or share your details. There is no advertising or analytics
   service on this site — no tracking pixel, no third-party script, and nothing
   loaded from another company's servers. When you browse TulipGlam, your
   browser talks to us and to nobody else.

   We keep order records because we have to: they are the receipt if something
   goes wrong. Everything else we will delete if you ask.

   ---
   Note: that last section is unusually strong and it is entirely true today —
   including the fonts, which are self-hosted, so no visitor's IP reaches Google
   in order to read your shop's name. If analytics is ever added, that paragraph
   has to change in the same commit that adds it.

   Note: the coming-soon email capture is LIVE. People are being asked for an
   address right now, which is why that paragraph is in the draft rather than
   waiting for launch.

4. THREE SMALLER ONES, NOT YET DRAFTED
===============================================================================

ORDER CONFIRMATION EMAIL COPY
   The templates exist and the outbox is queuing messages that will send the day
   SMTP is configured. Nobody has read the wording. Ten minutes, before it starts
   reaching customers.

GIFT CARD TERMS
   The page exists and is generic. It should say whether cards expire, whether
   they are refundable, and what happens to a balance after a partial spend.
   The code keeps a remaining balance indefinitely and never expires it. If the
   page says otherwise, the page is wrong, not the code.

REWARDS TERMS
   The rewards page states the three rules that matter: points confirm after
   7 days, the rate is locked at checkout, a new tier applies from the next
   order. It does NOT say that points expire after 12 months without a confirmed
   order, or that the shop can adjust a balance. Both are true in the code. Both
   should be visible before anyone has a balance worth arguing about.
